How do the controls work?

Hand controls for shooting: point your index finger to aim and fold your thumb to fire. On Overwatch Ridge, your eyes are also tracked: close one eye to look down the scope. Shoot a few targets to set your aim. Thumb gestures and the eye scope need no calibration.

Target Range warm-up: learn your aim by shooting, then land five accurate hits to start your selected game automatically. Mouse and touch remain available as an alternative.

Your camera stays local.

This browser interface talks to the Handy Python process running on this computer. Camera frames are processed in memory on your Mac, never uploaded to a cloud service or saved.

Camera modes may download MediaPipe models on first use. Mouse and touch modes work without a camera or model download. Keep the local server running while you play.
