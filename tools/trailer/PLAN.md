# The Handheld trailer

A 40-second trailer to post on X: 1920x1080, 60 fps, H.264 + AAC. It is cut to
music at **120 BPM**, so one beat is 0.5 s (30 frames) and one bar is 2 s
(120 frames). Every cut falls on a beat; most fall on a bar.

X autoplays videos **muted**, so the picture must tell the whole story by
itself: big, few words. With sound on, the music and the games' own sounds
hit together on the cuts.

## The story

"No controller. No keyboard. No mouse. Just your hands." on the beat; the sight
(the app's mark) snaps in and locks on. Bang: the four shooters in quick cuts.
"Now grab the wheel.": the sight's ring stays and its ticks swing into the spokes
of a steering wheel, which turns. Go: Grip Prix, four karts, four players on one
camera. The mark: **Handheld. Games you play with your hands.**

**No hand is drawn anywhere** (no illustrations, no glyphs, no 3D hands, no hand
skeleton picture in the games): the user rejected every drawn hand. The words, the
sight and the real games carry it.

| 12-17 | 0:22-0:34 | Grip Prix: GO on 22.0 (karts launch), a drift with sparks, four players split screen ("Up to 4 players. One camera."), a jump or an item, a night track, then half-bar cuts into the finish | **Second drop** at 22.0, bigger than the first; climax on bars 16-17 |

Game titles sit over the footage on each game's first bar (an eyebrow and a
name: "Finger Guns" over "High Noon"; "Up to 4 Players" over "Grip Prix").

## Who makes what (one owner per folder; nobody edits another's folder)

| The edit | `tools/trailer/edit.mjs` | `handheld-trailer.mp4`, `contact.jpg` |

A clip manifest entry: `{ "id", "game", "frames", "fps": 60, "slow": 1 | 2, "w": 1920, "h": 1080,
"what": "one line", "moments": [{ "t": seconds into the clip, "what": "a hit, an explosion..." }] }`.
Clips are captured a little longer than their slot (3-4 s for a 2 s slot) so the edit can pick
the in-point that puts the best moment on a beat.

## Rules for everyone

- **Never play a sound.** Headless Chrome only, always with `--mute-audio`. Never open a WAV or
  a video in a player. Check audio by analysis (levels, spectrograms, peaks), never by ear.
- The dev server is already running at `http://127.0.0.1:4901` (Vite, this checkout). Use it;
  do not stop it, and do not start another one on 4901.
- Frames are real: the games run through their own dev harnesses and are stepped exactly one
  video frame at a time (see `tools/promo/README.md`, `frames.mjs`, `capture.ts`). Nothing is
  faked or generated by an AI image or video model.
- Everything big goes in `tools/trailer/out/` (ignored by git). Do not commit.
- `npm run typecheck` and `npx vitest run` must still pass if you touch `src/`.
