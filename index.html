Your Camera Stays Here

Nintenthropic runs in your browser. Your camera picture is read on this computer to find your hand, and it is never saved or uploaded.

In Overwatch Ridge it also reads whether one of your eyes is closed, for the scope: only that, only in that game, and in Settings’ Eye Test when you start it. Nothing leaves this computer.

The first game with your hand downloads about 20 MB, once. After that, your browser keeps it.
